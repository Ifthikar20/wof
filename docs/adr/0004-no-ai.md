# ADR-0004: No AI or ML in the product

- **Status:** Accepted (product decision)
- **Date:** 2026-09-24

## Context
The product's value is *trustworthy, human, first-person* founder stories. AI-generated
content, AI moderation or opaque recommendation models would undermine that. They also
bring cost, privacy and explainability problems.

## Decision
No generative AI, no ML ranking, no ML moderation, no AI summarisation. Curation and
moderation use deterministic, documented rules plus human judgement:
- Digest ranking: a published gravity formula plus editor picks (`digest/curation.py`).
- Spam: explicit rules with named flags (`moderation/rules.py`).
- Verification: human review with a checklist.
We also ask AI crawlers not to train on the corpus (`robots.txt`).

## Consequences
- ✅ Every decision can be explained to a founder or reader ("held because: new_account_link").
- ✅ No model costs, no reader data sent to third parties.
- ⚠️ More human moderation effort. Mitigated by queues, throttles, Turnstile and verification gating.
- If this is ever revisited, it needs a new ADR with a privacy review.
